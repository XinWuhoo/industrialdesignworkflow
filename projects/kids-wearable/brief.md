# Kids wearable — brief (draft 0.1)

**Product:** a wrist-worn gadget for children: watch, tracker or call device (module function to be confirmed).
**User:** children aged 4–10 (assumed). Parents buy it; the child has to want to wear it.
**Must:** adjust to the wrist (110–160 mm circumference), be waterproof (5 ATM target: swimming, bath, rain), feel cute and modern.

## Assumptions to confirm
- Module about 36 × 32 × 12 mm: battery, PCB, display (round or square, about 24–26 mm).
- Ages 4–10. Children must be able to fasten and size it alone, without fine motor precision.
- Skin-contact parts in platinum-cured LSR silicone. No small detachable parts below choke-hazard size for the youngest end.
- Waterproofing comes from a sealed module (no open ports; pogo-pin or wireless charging).
